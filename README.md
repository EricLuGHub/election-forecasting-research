# Election Forecasting Research

Research tools for collecting, validating, and analyzing public election
forecast-market data.

This public export focuses on the research layer: scoped data collection,
market normalization, analysis, simulation, and tests. It intentionally omits
private data, device configuration, credentials, and deployment operations.

## Scope

- reproducible market-data snapshots;
- bounded research experiments;
- paper-only evaluation and risk checks;
- explicit assumptions and limitations.

This is research software, not investment advice or a claim of trading performance.
